One section per strategy; the section is inserted into sentence_variant.md as {strategy}.

## lexical
Lexical substitution: replace words with words of the same meaning and keep the sentence structure.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: FWB will provide 20 supply kits only if the commander allows access by Friday.

## passive
Active to passive voice: turn active clauses into passive ones, keeping who does what (the agent stays in a "by" phrase). Not applicable if the passage has no active clause with an agent.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: Twenty supply kits will be delivered by FWB only if access is permitted by the commander by Friday.

## clause_order
Clause order: move a condition, reason or other subordinate clause to the other side of the main clause. Not applicable if the passage has no clause that can move.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: Only if the commander permits access by Friday will FWB deliver 20 supply kits.

## split
Sentence splitting: split the passage into two or more sentences, so that a condition, reason or qualification sits in a different sentence from what it applies to, linked back by a phrase such as "this commitment" or "this". Not applicable if the passage cannot be split.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits. This commitment applies only if the commander permits access by Friday.

## register
Formal to plain register: say the same thing in plainer, more conversational words. Not applicable if the passage is already plain or conversational.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits, but only if the commander lets them in by Friday.

## conditional
Conditional reformulation: express a condition in a logically equivalent form, such as "X is a necessary condition for Y", "Y requires X" or "without X, no Y". Not applicable if the passage states no condition.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: Permission from the commander to access the site by Friday is a necessary condition for FWB's delivery of 20 supply kits.

## nominalise
Nominalisation: express actions as nouns ("delivery", "authorisation", "release") instead of verbs. Not applicable if the passage has no action verb that can be nominalised.
Example. Original: FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: FWB's delivery of 20 supply kits is conditional on the commander's authorization of access by Friday.

## pronoun
Explicit reference to pronoun: replace an explicit mention of a person, group or organisation with a pronoun, only where the passage or the text just before it names that referent so that the pronoun is unambiguous. Use the pronoun the document already uses for that referent, or "they" or "it". Do not add sentences. Not applicable if no mention can be replaced unambiguously.
Example. Original: The commander controls site access. FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if the commander permits access by Friday.
Variant: The commander controls site access. FWB will deliver 20 supply kits only if he permits access by Friday.
